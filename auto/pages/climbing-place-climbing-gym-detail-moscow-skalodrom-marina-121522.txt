About Us
Who we are and what we build.
Climbing Place and Hike Place are outdoor adventure platforms operated by Good Experience s.r.o. , a company based in Prague, Czechia. We build tools that help climbers and hikers discover routes, share beta, and connect with the community.
Our mission
We believe the best outdoor information comes from the people who actually climb and hike. Our platforms combine a searchable database of routes and trails with community ratings, reviews, photos, and practical details — approach times, grades, facilities, and more.
What we offer
Climbing Place — sport climbing, bouldering, via ferrata, and climbing gym route databases with interactive maps, topos, and gym wall layouts.
Hike Place — hiking trip reports with GPX tracks, difficulty ratings, and an interactive trail map.
Partner finder, news, tips, trainings, and a cross-domain chat so climbers and hikers can connect.
Community-driven
Route data, reviews, photos, gym layouts, and hiking reports are contributed and maintained by our users. We moderate submissions to keep the information accurate and useful.
