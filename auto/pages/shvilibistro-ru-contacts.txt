For kids
Dear parents,
No matter what the weather is outside, it is always bright and sunny in the restaurants of Shvili. Every weekend, we organize a special entertainment programme for all guests with children.
While adults enjoy the cozy atmosphere, socializing and favorite Georgian dishes, young visitors can take part in exciting master classes.
All our events are free, no booking in advance is required. Children aged 3 to 12 can participate.
The workshops take place on Saturdays and Sundays in Shvili restaurants.

Children’s eco magagine
Have you met Tisha the Tiger, the mascot of Shvili?
On this page, we have collected all stories about the tiger’s travels around the Anuyi National Park accompanied by his friends: a girl called Anyuta, a local Nanai boy called Neku and Arseny, a brave forester.
The Anuyi National Park was created to preserve the population of Amur tigers. Our restaurant takes an active part in the life of this unique natural area.
At the moment, there are about 40 tigers in the park, baby tigers have been born, and their number has been gradually increasing.
