About Yango Maps
Yango Maps is a free service where you can view city maps, find the places you need, and create routes to your destinations.
Yango Maps is available in browsers and as an app on mobile devices.
Log in to Yango Maps to get access to all the available features.
Configuring the map
Use Yango Maps features to change the layout, measure distances, and move around the map.
Search
Find places or organizations.
Places
Save your home and work addresses, create a list of favorite places, routes, and public transport stops.
Setting a route
Set a route to the desired object, choose the appropriate mode of transport, or check for parking lots nearby.
Reviews, photos, and fixes

Place card

Public transport routes and stops
