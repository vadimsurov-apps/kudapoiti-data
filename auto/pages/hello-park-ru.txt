Activities and attractions:
Multi-Level Soft Play Frame
Kidspace Romford is one of the largest, most exciting indoor play centres out there.
Its huge play frame spans several layers and is perfect for mini explorers.
Explore
The Thunderdome - Soft Ball Battle Arena
This is the real deal, the main event. Blast your friends in our soft ball arena...
Explore
Indoor Go Kart Track
Calling all little Lewis Hamiltons. Get behind the wheel and go for a spin in our very own indoor Go Kart track. One free ride on our go karts with your wristband. Additional rides £2.
Explore
The Climbing Zone - Interactive Climbing Walls
Height Restriction: You must be over 1.1m to use the climbing zone
Get ready to scale new heights! Race your friends to the top and see who conquers the wall first. The Climbing Zone is the ultimate test of speed, skill, and courage. Are you up for the challenge?
Explore
The Oceanarium - Interactive Virtual Aquarium
Step into a mesmerising under-sea world - no wetsuit required! Our interactive virtual aquarium brings glowing shrimp, scuttling crabs, floating whales, waving coral, and playful fish to life around you.
Touch the walls to make creatures dart and flip, then settle in for a breathtaking 3-minute journey from ocean depths up through the clouds and into space. Entry is included with your admission - don’t miss this enchanting aquatic adventure!
Explore
Toddler Village - Soft Play for Under 4s
Suitable for ages 4 and under, our Toddler Zone is a tot's paradise where they can climb, explore, role play and more. Our Soft Play zone a section designed specifically for smaller kids who want as much fun as the big kids!
Explore
Draw Alive - Interactive Drawing Game
Watch your drawings come to life with Draw Alive! With this interactive digital drawing game, children can colour in creatures and animals and watch them virtually come alive on a large screen with the help of our scanning technology.
Explore
Imagination Playground - Giant Building Block Fun
Using Imagination Playground blocks, kids build a new world every day. They make objects like animals, rocket ships, and robots. They make imaginary places like houses, factories, and cities. They make new dramatic scenarios, settings, and games to play. Most important, they make the rules. Because Imagination Playground is child-directed and open-ended, it encourages self-expression through deep, joyful play.
