For Kids
Dear parents,
No matter what kind of weather is outside, it is always sunny in Osteria Mario. Every weekend, we organize a special entertainment programme for all guests with children.
While the adults enjoy our cosy ambience, talk and taste their favourite Italian dishes, the younger guests can participate in our fun workshops.
All our events are free, no booking in advance is required. Children aged 3 to 12 can participate.
The workshops take place on Saturdays and Sundays in Osteria Mario restaurants.
