FAQ
Where are you located?
We’re located inside Riviera Shopping Mall, on the 3rd floor near the Auchan entrance.
You can walk to us from Avtozavodskaya metro station or the ZIL MCC station.
Coming by car? There’s a large, convenient parking garage with easy access from the Third Ring Road (TTK).
Can I leave my child at the park while I go shopping?
If your child is 6 or older, you’re welcome to leave them with us — we’ll make sure they’re safe and having fun.
For children aged 3 to 6, babysitting service is available upon request. Just ask our staff at the front desk for details.
Do you offer any discounts or special rates?
We offer discounts for large families and children with disabilities — just bring the appropriate documentation.
We also have special group offers available.
What should I bring to the park?
All you need is a pair of cotton socks. Forgot yours? No problem — socks are on sale at our gift shop.
There’s no need for indoor shoes: everyone plays in socks, which keeps the park clean and cosy for all our guests.
Can I celebrate my child’s birthday at your park?
We know that a great kids’ party brings joy to both children and their parents.
That’s why we’re happy to take care of everything — from table decorations and catering to entertainment and activities.
To see what we offer, just head to the “Parties” section of our site.
Can I buy a gift certificate?
Yes! A Hello Park Gift Certificate is a perfect present for any occasion.
You can choose a custom amount to cover any of our services — or go with a pre-set pass for 1, 5, or 10 visits.
Gift certificates are available for purchase at the park’s front desk.
