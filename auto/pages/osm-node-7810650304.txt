About the Museum
The Dead Dolls Museum is the world's first museum dedicated to scary dolls. Our exposition presents an impressive collection of gloomy puppet art: only here you will get acquainted with the works of the craziest puppeteers on the planet! A wide palette of puppet characters — from melancholic daydreaming to tragic doom and inhuman suffering, from slight infernality to sinister obsession and cruel brutality — will not leave indifferent even the most indifferent viewer to dolls.

Questions & Answers
Is your museum large?
The museum occupies XXX square meters. This is more than enough for a private doll museum, which, by its very concept, cannot and should not be large. From the very beginning, our goal has been to increase not the size of the museum, but the depth of its exhibition.
When is the best time to visit the museum?
Any time on weekdays. On weekends, we recommend visiting before 12:00 PM or after 8:00 PM. The museum is especially enjoyable when you have it all to yourself.
Do you offer discounts for students, large families, people with disabilities, or senior citizens?
No. We are a private museum and do not offer discounted admission.
How much time should I plan for my visit?
A visit with the audio guide usually takes 35 to 50 minutes. Without the audio guide, it takes 5 to 25 minutes, depending on your interest in the horror genre.
What is the minimum recommended age for visitors?
Our museum is rated 16+. Visitors under the age of 16 are welcome when accompanied by a parent or guardian. In practice, visitors under sixteen are quite rare, and those who do come are usually enthusiastic fans of the horror genre.
Is the museum very scary?
It depends on your experience with horror. If you do not watch horror films, you may well find the museum unsettling. It is important to understand that we are neither a haunted house nor an escape room, and our goal is not to frighten visitors.
Is photography allowed in the museum?
Photography and video recording are permitted, provided that no flash is used. Tripods and additional lighting equipment are not allowed.
How many dolls are in your collection?
Around 430 dolls.
Is it better to visit alone or with friends?
The museum is best experienced alone or with one other person. Larger groups tend to focus more on socializing than on exploring the exhibition.
Why should I use the audio guide?
The audio guide is the backbone of our exhibition. It tells the stories behind the most significant dolls, explains their origins, and explores their place in horror culture. Without it, the museum is experienced primarily as a collection of unusual exhibits.
