For Kids
Dear parents,
No matter what kind of weather is outside, it is always sunny in Osteria Mario. Every weekend, we organize a special entertainment programme for all guests with children.
While the adults enjoy our cosy ambience, talk and taste their favourite Italian dishes, the younger guests can participate in our fun workshops.
All our events are free, no booking in advance is required. Children aged 3 to 12 can participate.
The workshops take place on Saturdays and Sundays in Osteria Mario restaurants.

Children’s Eco Magazine
Are you already familiar with the symbol of the Osteria Mario children’s restaurant club – tiger cub Tisha?
This page contains all the travel stories of the tiger cub and his friends: the girl Anyuta, the Nanai boy Neku, the brave forester inspector Arseny.
Tiger cub Tisha lives in Anyui National Park, which, like many other parks and reserves, was specially created by people to preserve the number of endangered animal species such as Amur tigers, Kamchatka foxes, polar bears, snow leopards and many others.
Our restaurants are actively involved in the conservation of nature, the rescue of rare and endangered species of the world’s fauna. It is for this purpose that the Tigrus Restaurant Holding has established the Charitable Foundation «Conservation of the Natural Ecological Systems of the Far East and Siberia» .
